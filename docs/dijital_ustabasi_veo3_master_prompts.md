# 🎬 Google Veo 3 — Dijital Ustabaşı v1.0.5 Master Lansman Filmi Prompt Paketi

Bu doküman, **Dijital Ustabaşı (Bulut Oto Servis Sesli Teklif & Kurumsal PDF Platformu)** markası için **Google Veo 3** (veya Sora / Runway Gen-3 / Kling AI) yapay zeka video motorunda **en yüksek sinematik gerçekçilikte (4K Photorealistic)** reklam filmi üretmenizi sağlayan özel prompt paketidir.

---

## 🌟 1. Veo 3 Tek Parça Akışkan Film Master Prompt'u (Full Commercial)

> **Kullanım:** Google Veo 3'e tek bir metin kutusunda girilerek 60 saniyelik kesintisiz, akışkan reklam filmi oluşturulmasını sağlar.

```text
Cinematic 4K 60fps photorealistic commercial film for 'Dijital Ustabaşı' auto repair software. Opens in an authentic atmospheric auto repair workshop in Istanbul. A hard-working Turkish mechanic with greasy hands struggles to write prices on a torn paper invoice while an impatient customer waits. The camera smoothly dollies in as he wipes his hands and pulls out a sleek smartphone glowing with the 'Dijital Ustabaşı' app interface showing 05XX phone input and a green '7 GÜN ÜCRETSİZ USTA DENEMESİ' button. Camera orbits 360 degrees as he speaks into the phone microphone; glowing futuristic 3D orange and blue audio equalizer waves float around the phone, revealing two interactive UI buttons: amber '🛑 Durdur (Metne Dönüştür)' and emerald green '🚀 Durdur & Teklif Üret'. The scene transitions smoothly to a warm golden hour shot of the smiling mechanic sipping Turkish tea in his clean shop, holding a tablet displaying a professional corporate PDF quote with his workshop logo and green checkmark badge, accompanied by a horizontal action bar: '✅ Onayla', '❌ Reddet', '👁️ Önizle', '📥 İndir'. Anamorphic lens flares, shallow depth of field, professional lighting, film grain, highly detailed 8k render. --ar 16:9 --motion 5
```

## 🎥 2. Sahne Bazlı Veo 3 Video Prompt'ları (Modüler Prodüksiyon)

### 🎬 SAHNE 1: Sanayide Kağıt-Kalem Devrinin Bitişi (00:00 - 00:15)
* **Görsel:** Gerçekçi oto tamirhane ortamı, yağlı eller, yırtılan kağıt fatura, bekleyen sabırsız müşteri.
* **Veo 3 Prompt:**
```text
Cinematic 4k movie shot, 35mm anamorphic lens, slow motion dolly-in camera motion. Close-up of a Turkish auto mechanic with greasy hands in an authentic atmospheric repair garage. He holds a crumpled, oil-stained paper invoice with handwritten numbers, looking frustrated as the paper tears. In the blurred background, a customer impatiently checks his watch under warm workshop lighting. Volumetric dust, dramatic shadows, ultra-realistic motion, 60fps. --ar 16:9 --motion 4
```
* **Türkçe Seslendirme (Voiceover):**  
  > *"Sanayide zaman paradır ustanım! Müşteri beklerken kağıda kalemle fiyat yazmakla, hesap kitapla vakit kaybetme! Kağıt-kalem devri bitti!"*
